About Kota Kedharnadh

Undergraduate AI & DS Engineer bridging algorithmic machine learning with physical robotics, quantum computing, and embedded telemetry.

Engineering Background

B.Tech AI & Data Science • Amrita Univ, Coimbatore

Published IEEE Author

I am an Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS) scholar at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore (2023 - Present). My goal is to build intelligent physical and cloud systems that solve real human safety, agriculture, and healthcare challenges.

My engineering work ranges from constructing subterranean Coal Mine Threat Detection Rovers using YOLO vision models, Raspberry Pi, and gas telemetry, to evaluating Variational Quantum Circuits (Qiskit VQC) for medical diagnostic predictions.

I have published peer-reviewed research on Webots Autonomous Navigation in IEEE Xplore, showcasing my passion for combining simulation environment testing with practical robotics execution.

Core Specialization Domains:

Computer Vision & ML

YOLO v8/v9, OpenCV processing, Scikit-Learn, PyTorch fundamentals, custom object detection, image segmentation.

Quantum Computing

Qiskit VQC circuits, quantum encodings (Amplitude, Angle, Basis, IQP), variational quantum classification, ansatz optimization.

Embedded & IoT

ESP32, Raspberry Pi 4, Arduino, Methane (MQ-4) & CO2 gas sensors, soil moisture telemetry, Flask REST APIs.

Autonomous Navigation

Webots robotics simulation, sweep coverage algorithms, obstacle avoidance, LiDAR point clouds, ROS fundamentals.

Research Focus: Simulation-first robotics testing and hybrid quantum-classical algorithms.

In my published IEEE paper on Webots autonomous navigation, I analyzed sweep coverage and obstacle detection performance under simulated real-world conditions. My current research explores how Quantum Machine Learning can improve multi-dimensional diagnostic classification while maintaining minimal circuit depth.
